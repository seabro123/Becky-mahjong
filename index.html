How to play

Four players share one device. The starting player draws, then discards one non-wild tile. Players may call that discard to make an exposed group of 3–6 matching tiles. Exposures are made only by calling a discard.

Jokers and blank tiles are wild. They can fill missing tiles in calls and your saved hand pattern. Flowers and seasons, and Charleston, are not included.

Set up a hand pattern before starting. The pattern editor describes the 14 tiles needed to win; wild tiles can fill any missing requirement. This is a customizable home game, not an official NMJL card validator.
